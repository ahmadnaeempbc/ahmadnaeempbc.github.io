My first six days on any plant

By Muhammad Ahmad Naeem · 21 November 2026

My fastest wins in plants do not start with tools. They start with clarity. In my first six days on any plant I build a working manual of how the place really runs. The one I wrote for a beverage plant came to 66 pages.

Map the process

I follow the product from raw materials to the pallet across every major line, and write down each step in the order it happens.

Decode the mechanics

For each machine I want the mechanical concept behind what it does, and not just the list of steps. If I understand why it works, I can work out why it stops.

Capture the parameters

Pressures, temperatures, speeds and the checkpoints that matter during real operation and troubleshooting. These are the numbers people ask for on shift.

Connect the utilities

Water, compressed air, CO2, syrup and the recovery loops. Production runs on utilities, and many line problems start there.

Make it usable on shift

The manual is visual, so it can be used standing next to the machine and not only at a desk.

The value is simple. Once the manual exists, I do not spend the rest of the year learning the same things again or asking the same questions twice.
