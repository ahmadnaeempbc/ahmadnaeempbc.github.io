From GIK to a Sydney plant floor

By Muhammad Ahmad Naeem · 21 November 2026

From beverage to beverage. I crossed an ocean and still ended up standing beside production lines.

Where it started

I studied mechanical engineering at GIK Institute in Pakistan and graduated in 2024. My interest in factories began before that, in 2022, as a maintenance intern at Unilever in Lahore. That was the first time I saw how much engineering sits behind an ordinary product on a shelf.

PepsiCo

After graduating I joined PepsiCo as a production intern and became a Production Engineer. I was responsible for production, operations and maintenance on two PET bottling lines and a Tetra Pak juice line, with a production team of 80. My days were measured in uptime, breakdowns and decisions that could not wait.

Why I went back to study

That world sharpened execution and ownership. It also made one thing clear. I did not want to solve the same problems faster. I wanted to learn how to eliminate them. In February 2026 I moved to Sydney for the Master of Professional Engineering at the University of Technology Sydney.

Elgas

My first Australian project was with Elgas, through UTS, designing automation for LPG cylinder cage handling. A different product, but the same thinking: flow, repeatability, downtime and safety.

Refresco

In 2026 I joined Refresco Australia in Sydney as a Manufacturing Engineering Intern, back beside fillers, blow moulders and conveyors. A different plant, different machines, the same questions.

Beverage lines taught me three things. Anything can happen, anywhere on the line, at any moment. What you see is rarely where it started. And when something goes wrong, you have to look beyond the obvious to find out why.

Understand the machine. Improve the process. Learn from the people who run it. And always ask why one more time.
