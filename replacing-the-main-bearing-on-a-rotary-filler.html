Replacing the main bearing on a rotary filler

By Muhammad Ahmad Naeem · 21 November 2026

A machine does not fail suddenly. It warns you quietly, through vibration. On one of our lines a KRONES rotary filler started showing abnormal vibration and instability, and we traced it to the main bearing.

Why it could not wait

A worn main bearing looks like a small mechanical issue. Left alone it disturbs filling performance, causes repeat stoppages, wears the parts connected to it and ends in unplanned downtime at the worst possible time.

Confirm before you open the machine

We verified the problem through the vibration behaviour and the symptoms on the machine before committing to a shutdown. Opening a filler carousel is a big job, so the diagnosis has to be right.

Plan the shutdown properly

The plan covered safety, hygiene controls, tooling, lifting and spares. A filler is a hygienic machine, so everything that comes out has to go back clean, and nothing can be left behind.

The work itself

We opened the carousel and worked through tight access, heavy parts and precision fits. The worn bearing was replaced and the machine was rebuilt with alignment checked and verified before restart.

Treat a bearing change like this as a reliability job and not a quick fix. The patience and coordination it takes are the part most people never see, and the credit belongs to the whole team that did it.
